Chatbot

Thanks! I looked into it, and here is what I found. Your checkout works in four steps.

First, the Cart step adds up the items and shipping. This step works correctly on every screen size. Next, the Address step saves where the order should go, and it also works on every screen size.

Then comes the Payment step, and this is where the problem is. On phones, the cookie bar is fixed to the bottom of the screen and its z-index puts it on top of the Pay button. Most of the button is covered, so people can't tap it.

Finally, the Receipt step confirms the order. However, phone customers never reach it, because they can't get past the Payment step.

Let me know if you'd like more detail about any of these steps, or if you'd like me to go ahead and fix it.

3. Tap to decide

See each option. Tap one.

Chatbot

Great question! There are a few ways we could fix this. Here are your options:

Option 1: Sticky Pay button. We could stick the Pay button to the bottom of the screen and rest the cookie bar just above it. The advantage is that the button is always in reach. The downside is that the bar and button together take up more of the screen.

Option 2: Cookie bar on top. We could move the cookie bar to the top of the page. This is the smallest change. However, it can push the page content down and may cover your header.

Option 3: Cookie pop-up. We could replace the bar with a pop-up in the middle of the screen. The Pay button stays clear afterwards, but the pop-up blocks the page until it is dismissed.

Overall, I would recommend Option 1. Let me know which one you'd prefer and I'll get started!
